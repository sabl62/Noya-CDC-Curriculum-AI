# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Noya is for Nepal Grade 10 students preparing from CDC textbooks, especially students who need faster explanations, chapter-aware notes, and exam-focused answers without leaving the textbook.

## Product Purpose

Noya turns official textbook study into an interactive AI tutor experience. Students select a subject and chapter, read or select textbook text, and ask Noya for grounded explanations, solutions, notes, and revision help.

## Brand Personality

Focused, precise, calm, and quietly ambitious. Noya should feel like a premium study desk: serious enough for exams, simple enough for daily use, and smart without sounding inflated.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic neon AI dashboards, childish education mascots, loud cartoon gradients, repetitive feature-card grids, vague AI claims, and boring SaaS landing-page templates.

## Design Principles

- Show the actual study workflow instead of abstract AI decoration.
- Make textbook trust visible without clutter.
- Keep the tone premium, direct, and student-centered.
- Use motion to clarify and guide, not to distract.
- Design for confidence before hype.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, responsive layouts, keyboard-friendly actions, and reduced-motion alternatives. Copy should be direct and understandable for students.
